Troubleshooting

Common questions

I cannot sign in

Use the same email address and password as your My NPPE Academia account. Check for typing errors and confirm that your device has an internet connection.

If you have forgotten your password, enter your email on the sign-in screen and tap Forgot password? Follow the reset email, then return to the app. Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive.

The app says “Mobile plan required”

The app requires an active account with a mobile entitlement. Website access alone does not necessarily include mobile access.

If you believe your account already includes it, tap Refresh access. If the message remains, sign out and sign back in, then contact support with your account email. We can check the entitlement attached to your account.

The app says “No active access”

Your account may have expired, or the app may not have retrieved its current status. Check your internet connection and tap Refresh access.

If the status seems incorrect, contact support. Do not create a second account to troubleshoot an existing account.

Questions or results will not load

Check your connection, close and reopen the app, and try again. If the issue persists, tell us the screen name, question or practice-exam number, and any error message shown.

For a timed mock, record the issue and contact support before clearing app storage, so you do not lose your saved session.

How do I resume a saved session?

Return to the practice area where you started the session and use its resume option when a saved attempt is available.

In-progress sessions are saved on the device. Reinstalling the app, clearing its data or switching devices can remove or leave behind that local save. Avoid those steps until you have finished the session or contacted support.

The AI tutor is unavailable

The tutor requires Full-plan access and is subject to a daily request limit. Check the message shown in the app; reaching the limit can temporarily prevent further requests.

Responses use a third-party AI service and may contain errors. Avoid entering sensitive personal information, and verify important study points against reliable sources. For a technical error, send support the error message and time it occurred.

I think an answer or explanation is incorrect

Email us the question wording or identifier, its topic, the answer shown and why you believe it needs review. Include the relevant jurisdiction or source if available.

Legal and regulatory requirements can vary by province and change over time. We welcome reports that help improve the study materials.

My progress looks different on another device

Check that you are signed in to the same account. Completed activity and device-local in-progress sessions can be stored differently, so an unfinished attempt may not appear on another device.

If completed results appear to be missing, contact support with the activity, approximate completion time and devices involved.

Account & data

Deleting your account

In the mobile app, open Settings → Delete Account, enter DELETE when prompted, then confirm deletion. This permanently removes your account and study history and cannot be undone.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your online account. If you cannot sign in or complete the deletion process, email support from your account’s email address and request account-deletion assistance.

Some transaction or legally required records may be retained as explained in our privacy policy. You can also find account export and privacy controls in the website’s Settings page after signing in.

Accessibility and feedback

If the app is difficult to use with VoiceOver, TalkBack, larger text or other accessibility settings, please tell us. Include the screen involved, device details and what would make it easier to use.
