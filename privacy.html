How TechnologiesRehman handles information for the My NPPE Academia mobile study app and its connected account services.

Effective October 4, 2026 · TechnologiesRehman · Ontario, Canada

1. Who we are and what this covers

TechnologiesRehman operates My NPPE Academia, a study resource for Canadian engineering and geoscience candidates preparing for the National Professional Practice Examination. This policy covers the mobile app, the account and study services it connects to, and requests sent to our support team.

Our website also provides registration and account services. See the website privacy policy for additional information about website use. This page provides the mobile-specific information below.

2. Information we handle

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, authentication information and profile details such as province or territory when provided during registration. Our authentication provider processes passwords; support never needs your password.
  • Study records: quiz and mock-exam sessions, selected answers, scores, completion times and domain performance associated with your account.
  • Access and purchase records: plan, access expiry and mobile entitlement. Website purchase records may include transaction identifiers, amount, purchaser name and card last four digits. Where Apple purchases are available, our server verifies Apple transaction identifiers, product, purchase date, environment, account association, access period and refund or revocation status. Apple processes in-app payment; Stripe processes website card payments. Our app does not ask you to enter full card details.
  • Tutor information: questions and conversation text you submit, relevant quiz context, and account-linked request counts used to enforce daily limits. See the AI Tutor section.
  • Support information: contact details, messages, device/app details and any screenshots or attachments you choose to send us.
  • Technical information: network requests to our service providers include information such as IP address and request metadata. Operational and security logs may be handled by those providers.

The app also stores sign-in session information, appearance preferences and saved practice-session state locally on your device. Some completed study records are stored in your online account. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove local preferences and unfinished attempts.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, check access, deliver lessons and practice tools, save study results, show progress, answer tutor requests, enforce usage limits, provide support and protect the service against misuse. We also use necessary transaction records for accounting and payment-related disputes.

The mobile app does not contain advertising or advertising-tracking features. We do not sell personal information or use mobile study data for cross-company targeted advertising.

4. AI Tutor and Anthropic

When you send a tutor question, the app sends the conversation text and relevant study-question context to our server. Our server uses Anthropic's API to generate a response. Relevant context can include the question, answer options, your selected answer and the explanation being discussed. Our tutor integration does not intentionally send your account email or password to Anthropic.

Our application database records account-linked tutor usage, rather than storing a persistent tutor conversation history. This does not mean that Anthropic immediately deletes the content it processes.

Anthropic describes standard API input/output retention of up to 30 days, with exceptions for legal obligations, policy enforcement or a different applicable agreement. Provider processing is governed by its terms and privacy practices. Read Anthropic's privacy policy and its commercial API retention information.

Do not include confidential client material, passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information in tutor prompts. You can choose not to use the tutor and continue using the other features included in your access. AI explanations can be incorrect; check important statements against authoritative sources.

5. Service providers and disclosures

  • Supabase provides authentication and database services for accounts, study records and access information.
  • Vercel hosts the website and server endpoints used by the app.
  • Stripe processes website payments and associated transaction information.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases where offered and provides signed transaction and refund information to verify and restore access. Apple handles payment details under its own privacy practices.
  • Anthropic processes tutor content as described above.
  • GitHub hosts this public privacy page and our support page. Visiting them creates normal web requests to GitHub; they do not require an NPPE account.

We disclose information to service providers as needed to deliver these functions, and when necessary to comply with lawful requests, protect rights or address fraud and security incidents. Information may be processed outside your province or Canada and may be subject to the laws of the processing location.

6. Retention and deletion

Account and study records are retained while needed to provide your account and study history. Account deletion removes your authentication account, profile, quiz sessions, answers and tutor usage records from the active application databases.

Limited purchase information can remain after deletion for accounting, legal obligations, fraud prevention and chargeback handling. These records can include your email, former account identifier, purchaser name, card last four digits, plan, amount, transaction identifier and transaction dates. Apple purchase records may retain the original account-association identifier, transaction/product identifiers, dates and revocation status after the active account association is removed. Retained transaction records do not keep your study account active. Contact us for information about retention applicable to your records.

Service-provider logs, backups and content already sent to third parties may remain subject to those providers' retention practices and applicable legal obligations. Deleting an account does not necessarily erase all such copies immediately. Uninstalling the app does not delete your online account.

7. Your privacy choices

  • Access or export: use the export control in your website account settings, or contact us to request access to your information.
  • Correct information: contact us from your account email to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account: in the mobile app, open Settings → Delete Account, enter DELETE and confirm. This is permanent. If you cannot sign in or cannot reach Settings, email us from your account email for assistance. We may verify your identity before processing a request.
  • Local data: you can remove locally stored data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app; this can also remove unfinished sessions.
  • AI Tutor: choose not to submit tutor requests if you do not want that content processed by Anthropic.

You may ask questions about processing, withdraw consent where applicable, or make a privacy complaint using the contact details below. Some information is necessary to operate an account or meet legal obligations.

8. Security and intended audience

We use encrypted network connections, authentication and account-scoped access controls to help protect information. No system guarantees complete security. Protect your device and credentials, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

The service is intended for professional licensure candidates and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to this service.

9. Policy changes

We update this page when our practices change and show the current effective date above. Where required, we provide notice or obtain consent for material changes.
